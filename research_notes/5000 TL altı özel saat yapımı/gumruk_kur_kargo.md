# Turkish customs, exchange rates and shipping for small watch-part imports from China (as of 7 Oct 2026)

Scope: a private individual in Turkey importing small, low-value (<~€100) watch parts for personal use from AliExpress, JLCPCB, JLC3DP/JLCCNC and PCBWay. Research date: 7 October 2026. Several official pages (ticaret.gov.tr SSS, PwC, Verginet, Şikayetvar detail pages) returned 503/403 to the fetch tool. Where a fact came only through a search-engine snippet of such a page, it is marked "(via search snippet)".

**Headline for the report writer:** Turkey's €30 de-minimis/flat-tax regime for postal and express e-commerce parcels **was abolished for general goods** by Presidential Decision (Cumhurbaşkanı Kararı) No. 10813. The decision was published in Resmî Gazete No. 33130 on 7 Jan 2026 and took legal effect on 6 Feb 2026. The Ministry's own administrative cut-off was 1 Feb 2026. Since then, every non-medicine parcel to an individual, even one worth €1, goes through **normal import procedures**. That means a detailed declaration (detaylı beyan), normal tariff duty plus KDV (and ÖTV where listed), stamp duty, and in practice a customs-broker fee. That fixed overhead is roughly **5,600–6,000 TL per parcel via PTT** and anecdotally **10,000–20,000 TL via DHL/FedEx**. It dwarfs the value of a typical €20–100 watch-parts order. Many popular news sites still quote "%30 EU / %60 non-EU". The legal texts and expert bulletins indicate that this flat rate now survives **only for prescription medicines and food supplements**.

## 1. Current (2026) threshold for postal/express e-commerce shipments: legal source and effective dates

### Takeaway
There is **no value threshold any more** for general goods. CK 10813 (RG 7 Jan 2026, No. 33130; in force 6 Feb 2026) deleted the €30 category from the flat-tax article (Art. 62) and from the simplified-declaration article (Art. 126(1)(b)) of the Implementing Decision on Customs Law No. 4458. The only remaining low-value simplified regime covers prescription medicines and supplements up to €1,500. Books, samples/models and some other categories are also carved out. No reversal had been found as of early Oct 2026.

### Cited Findings
- **Legal instrument.** The 7 Jan 2026 Resmî Gazete (No. 33130) lists "4458 Sayılı Gümrük Kanununun Bazı Maddelerinin Uygulanması Hakkında Kararda Değişiklik Yapılmasına Dair Karar (Karar Sayısı: 10813)" under Cumhurbaşkanı Kararları — [Resmî Gazete index, 7 Ocak 2026](https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2026/01/20260107.htm) (fetched 7 Oct 2026).
- **What it changed (Art. 62(1), flat tax).** The old text covered "kıymeti 30 Avro'yu geçmeyen eşya" plus medicines up to €1,500. The new text covers only medicines and food supplements arriving "bir sağlık kuruluşu raporu veya doktor reçetesine istinaden", worth up to €1,500. "The 30 EUR general category for goods is removed from the new text." — [KPMG Vergi bulletin on CK 10813](https://kpmgvergi.com/yayinlar/mali-bultenler/gumruk/gumruk-kanununun-bazi-maddelerinin-uygulanmasi-hakkinda-kararda-degisiklik-yapilmasina-dair-karar-yayimlanmistir/3457) (Jan 2026).
- **What it changed (Art. 126(1)(b), declaration).** Old: goods "değeri 30 Avro'yu aşan ancak 1500 Avro'yu aşmayan". New: goods "değeri 1500 Avro'yu aşmayan". So everything up to €1,500 is now declared in the normal way. Effective 06.02.2026 (30 days after publication) — [KPMG Vergi](https://kpmgvergi.com/yayinlar/mali-bultenler/gumruk/gumruk-kanununun-bazi-maddelerinin-uygulanmasi-hakkinda-kararda-degisiklik-yapilmasina-dair-karar-yayimlanmistir/3457). The same old/new Art. 62 summary, with 30%/60% rates now attached only to medicines and supplements, appears in [PwC Gümrük Bülteni 2026/28](https://www.pwc.com.tr/tr/hizmetlerimiz/vergi/dolayli-vergi/bultenler/gumruk-bultenleri/2026/4458-sayili-gumruk-kanununun-bazi-maddelerinin-uygulanmasi-hakkinda-kararda-degisiklik-yapilmasina-dair-karar-10813.html) (via search snippet; the page returned 403).
- **Customs-broker reading.** Goods of €30 or less are no longer subject to the flat duty. They fall under "mevzuat genel hükümleri çerçevesinde yürürlükte olan ithalat vergilerine ilişkin oranlar". Art. 126(1)(b) now covers "Normal usulde beyan edilecek" non-commercial individual goods up to €1,500. Effective 6/2/2026 — [EN Gümrük Müşavirliği blog, 7 Jan 2026](https://engumruk.com/blog/posta-ve-hizli-kargo-tasimaciliginda-30-avro-limiti-kaldirildi-yurtdisi-kaynakli-internet-sitelerinden-yapilan-alisverisler).
- **Ministry of Trade press release, 7 Jan 2026.**
  - The simplified customs declaration (Basitleştirilmiş Gümrük Beyannamesi, BGB) for overseas e-commerce ends for all products regardless of value, "1 Şubat 2026 tarihinden itibaren geçerli olmak üzere".
  - Goods up to €30 are to be imported "bundan böyle mevzuatın genel hükümlerine göre normal ithalat prosedürleri çerçevesinde".
  - A 20 Oct 2025 circular had already removed toys, shoes and stationery from BGB.
  - Still under simplified treatment: prescription medicines and supplements ≤€1,500; test/analysis items, samples and models for manufacturers/exporters; books and printed matter; diplomatic goods; passenger effects; educational and scientific items.
  - Rationale: 81% of 182 tested cross-border products were non-compliant.
  - Source: [Ticaret Bakanlığı basın açıklaması, 07.01.2026](https://ticaret.gov.tr/haberler/e-ithalatta-basitlestirilmis-gumruk-beyannamesi-kapsaminin-degistirilmesine-iliskin-yeni-duzenleme-hakkinda-basin-aciklamasi).
- **Two dates.** The Ministry's press release gives 1 Feb 2026 for ending BGB. A Ministry circular titled "2026/4 Sayılı Genelge – 2022/9 Sayılı Genelgede Değişiklik" exists ([PDF link](https://ticaret.gov.tr/data/695e56a213b8762ee8546909/2026-4%20Say%C4%B1l%C4%B1%20Genelge%202022_9%20Say%C4%B1l%C4%B1%20Genelgede%20De%C4%9Fi%C5%9Fiklik_Ustyazi.pdf), title only; the download failed). It is probably the administrative vehicle for that date, but this is my inference. The legal effective date of CK 10813 is 6 Feb 2026. Media report both dates. One Milliyet article also gives the decision date as 6 Jan 2026, which is the signing date — [Milliyet, 8 Jan 2026](https://www.milliyet.com.tr/ekonomi/yurt-disindan-e-ticarette-gumruksuz-donem-bitti-7517209); [Webrazzi, 7 Jan 2026](https://webrazzi.com/2026/01/07/yurt-disi-alisverislerde-gumruk-muafiyeti-kaldirildi-30-euro-limiti-tarihe-karisti/).
- **History (older, for context).**
  - The limit was cut from €150 to €30 (Aug 2024), then effectively to about €27 once shipping was counted in the value (Dec 2024) — [Milliyet, 8 Jan 2026](https://www.milliyet.com.tr/ekonomi/yurt-disindan-e-ticarette-gumruksuz-donem-bitti-7517209).
  - A 2024 vendor article says that in Aug 2024 the non-EU flat rate rose to 60% and the postal/express threshold fell from €150 to €30 — [prototippcb.com.tr (2024)](https://prototippcb.com.tr/317/jlcpcb-ve-pcbway-g%C3%BCmr%C3%BCk-pcb-s%C4%B1n%C4%B1r%C4%B1-30%E2%82%AC-s%C4%B1n%C4%B1r%C4%B1na-%C3%A7ekildi).
- **Books.** Books under €150 by post or express stay exempt without a declaration, and books from €150 to €1,500 are declared at 0% — [Prof. Dr. Ersan Şen on X, Jan 2026](https://x.com/ProfDrErsanSen/status/2008853137432494364). Not relevant to watch parts, but it shows the carve-out pattern.
- **Possible softening, not enacted.** AK Parti spokesman Ömer Çelik hinted at a possible exception for technology-purpose products. Coverage presents it as a signal, not a rule — [DonanımHaber, "AK Parti'den … geri adım sinyali"](https://www.donanimhaber.com/ak-parti-den-yurt-disi-alisveris-duzenlemesinde-geri-adim-sinyali--200796) (via search snippet).
- **No reversal found.** A Sept 2026 search found no reinstatement of the €30 exemption. Sept 2026 articles still describe it as abolished — [Hürriyet bilgi page](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/bilgi/galeri/yurt-disi-gumruk-vergisi-siniri-2026-ne-kadar-oldu-dustu-mu-30-euro-siniri-kalkti-mi-43078592); [Cumhuriyet](https://www.cumhuriyet.com.tr/turkiye/yurt-disi-alisveris-30-euro-siniri-kalkti-mi-yurt-disi-gumruk-vergisi-siniri-2026-ne-kadar-oldu-2468169).

### Inferences
- For watch parts (Chapter 91, or Chapter 85 for quartz electronics), the relevant regime since 6 Feb 2026 is **normal import with a detailed declaration**, even for €1 items.
- The "€1,500 / 30 kg" figures many blogs still quote are now the ceiling for an individual's non-commercial **normal-procedure** declaration through the carrier (Art. 126(1)(b)). They are no longer a tax-free or flat-tax band.
- The "numune/model" carve-out is for manufacturers, exporters and recognised education/R&D projects. It is not generally available to a private hobbyist (see Q3).

### Gaps

- I could not read the content of the Jan 2026 "Posta ve Hızlı Kargo … Tebliğ (Seri No: 5)" amendment. Verginet blocked access ([Verginet Gümrük Sirküleri 2026-2](https://www.verginet.net/dtt/11/Gumruk-Sirkuleri-2026-2.aspx), title only).

## 2. Tax rates, ÖTV on watches/parts, and what actually happens to a parcel (PTT vs DHL/UPS/FedEx, fees)

### Takeaway
- **Media "30% / 60%" figures are outdated for general goods.** After 6 Feb 2026 a watch-part parcel from China pays normal taxes:
  - tariff duty for its GTİP (plus any İlave Gümrük Vergisi, İGV, for China origin),
  - **KDV 20%** on CIF + duties,
  - ÖTV only if the GTİP is on ÖTV List IV,
  - plus fixed costs: stamp duty and a broker fee.
- **ÖTV.** Watches and watch parts are generally **not** on List IV. The only Chapter 91 line is **9113.90.00.30.00**: straps made of pearl, precious, synthetic or reconstructed stones, at 20%. The law states explicitly that parts of listed goods are excluded.
- **Fixed overhead.** Via PTT it is about **3,984 TL broker fee (3,320 TL + KDV) + 1,650 TL stamp duty ≈ 5,634 TL** before taxes. DHL/FedEx anecdotes run to **10,000–20,000 TL** per small parcel.

### Cited Findings
**Rates (law and commentary)**
- **Post-2026 rate structure.**

  - General goods now pay "mevzuatın genel hükümleri" import tax rates — [EN Gümrük, 7 Jan 2026](https://engumruk.com/blog/posta-ve-hizli-kargo-tasimaciliginda-30-avro-limiti-kaldirildi-yurtdisi-kaynakli-internet-sitelerinden-yapilan-alisverisler).

  - The only watch line is "9113.90.00.30.00 (Saat kayışları) İnci, kıymetli taş; sentetik veya terkip yoluyla elde edilen taşlardan mamul olanlar — 20".

  - Source: [4760 sayılı ÖTV Kanunu, consolidated text on mevzuat.gov.tr (PDF), List IV p. 57](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.4760.pdf) (downloaded 7 Oct 2026; consolidation includes 2025 amendments).
- **KDV.** Plastic watch straps (9113.90.00.40.00) take the general **20%** KDV on import and supply, per a GİB özelge of 20/09/2024 — [Kızılkaya blog on the özelge](https://www.kizilkaya.com.tr/blog/9113-90-00-40-00-gtip-numarali-esyanin-ithalinde-ve-tesliminde-kdv-orani).

  - 9108 (complete movements), 9110 (unassembled movements), 9111 (cases), 9112, 9113 (straps: .10 precious metal, .20 base metal, .90 other incl. leather/plastic), 9114.30 (dials), 9114.40 (plates and bridges), 9114.90.10 (springs incl. mainsprings), 9114.90.90 (other parts).
  - Source: [gumruk.com.tr Fasıl 91 listing](https://www.gumruk.com.tr/91/gtip). Rates on that site are paywalled.

### Inferences
**Landed-cost formula for a watch-part parcel from China, post-6 Feb 2026, PTT route.** Amounts in TL; FX = TCMB forex selling rate (see Q6).
- CIF = (goods + shipping [+ insurance]) × FX
- Duty = CIF × (MFN rate + İGV for GTİP; exact rate unknown, see Gaps)
- KDV = 20% × (CIF + duty [+ ÖTV])
- Fixed = 1,650 TL stamp duty + 3,984 TL broker fee (incl. KDV) ≈ 5,634 TL. Add any ardiye, plus about 780 TL if a noter vekaleti is required.

**Worked examples** at EUR selling 55.0898 TL (TCMB, 7 Oct 2026):

| Order (goods + shipping) | CIF (TL) | Duty assumed | KDV (TL) | Total add-on (TL) | Landed (TL) | Multiplier |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| €40 | 2,204 | 0% | 441 | ≈ 6,075 | ≈ 8,280 | 3.8× |
| €40 | 2,204 | 10% | 485 | ≈ 6,340 | ≈ 8,540 | 3.9× |
| €100 | 5,509 | 0% | 1,102 | ≈ 6,736 | ≈ 12,245 | 2.2× |

The DHL/FedEx route is worse: anecdotally 10,000–20,000 TL of add-ons.

**Decision rule.** Buying locally is cheaper whenever the local price is below (import price × ~1.2 × (1+duty)) + ~5,600 TL (PTT) or + ~10,000–15,000 TL (express).
- For a project budget under 5,000 TL, **any single direct import parcel that goes through customs will on its own exceed the budget**, unless the seller or platform clears customs as importer of record (Temu/WhaleCo-style, see Q4) or the parcel slips through uncontrolled. Uncontrolled passage is not a plan.
- Consolidating all parts into **one parcel** is essential, because the fixed costs are per declaration.
- The duty rate itself is a minor factor next to the fixed costs. Even a 10% duty adds only about 2–5% to the total landed cost at these values.

## 3. Is there an annual or monthly per-person limit on the number of shipments?

### Takeaway
I found no annual per-person cap. A **5-shipments-per-calendar-month** rule exists. DHL now applies it to the model-exemption (education/R&D) channel, and a 2026 secondary guide lists it as a general postal/express rule. After Feb 2026, general goods go through normal declarations, so the practical limit is the "ticari miktar/mahiyet" (commercial quantity/character) test rather than a parcel count.

### Cited Findings
- DHL (individuals, model exemption for school, university, foundation or TGB R&D projects): "her bir gönderi için maksimum 30 EUR (navlun dâhil) değer limiti" and "bir takvim ayında aynı kişi adına en fazla 5 gönderiye izin verilmektedir" — [DHL Kategori 3 İşlemleri](https://gumruk.dhl.com.tr/kategori-3-islemleri-sahislar). Older DHL text applied the 5-per-month cap to simplified individual clearance generally, with excess shipments needing detaylı beyan — [gumruk.dhl.com.tr](https://gumruk.dhl.com.tr/) (via search snippet; older page).
- A 2026 guide lists "maximum 5 shipments per calendar month per person" as a postal/express rule — [Lires rehber, 8 Apr 2026](https://www.lires.com.tr/post/posta-ve-h%C4%B1zl%C4%B1-kargo-muafiyeti-rehberi-2026) (secondary; may describe the pre-Feb-2026 BGB regime).
- A customs consultancy describes the "MODEL" channel for R&D and education as €30 per shipment and 5 shipments per month — [Köyceğiz Gümrük, Numune-Model 2026](https://koycegizgumruk.com/blog/numune-model-ayrimi-2026-genelge-detayli-rehber) (via search snippet).
- Individual imports must be "ticari miktar ve mahiyet arz etmeyen" to use the individual normal-declaration channel up to €1,500 — [EN Gümrük](https://engumruk.com/blog/posta-ve-hizli-kargo-tasimaciliginda-30-avro-limiti-kaldirildi-yurtdisi-kaynakli-internet-sitelerinden-yapilan-alisverisler).
- One AliExpress user reported getting a customs-broker email when ordering more than five pieces. This is a single, unverified report — [Şikayetvar AliExpress vergi şikayetleri](https://www.sikayetvar.com/aliexpress/vergi-ucreti/gumruk-vergisi) (via search snippet).

### Inferences
- For a private watch hobbyist the monthly count matters less than (a) each parcel triggering its own fixed fees and (b) multiple identical parts (e.g. 10 identical movements) being judged "ticari miktar". That would push the import into commercial procedures, possibly with TAREKS/TSE.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm the current legal basis of the 5-per-month rule (Tebliğ article) post-Feb 2026, or whether it still applies to anything an individual hobbyist would use.

## 4. AliExpress tax handling in 2026, compared with Temu and Trendyol

### Takeaway
- **AliExpress.** It is still accessible and still ships to Turkey. It often collects a "vergi" line at checkout, but this does **not** reliably prevent a second charge on arrival. Many Feb–Mar 2026 Şikayetvar complaints describe PTT or broker demands of about 5,500 TL in declaration fees after tax had already been paid at checkout. I found no evidence that AliExpress has become importer of record in Turkey.
- **Temu.** It closed cross-border sales to Turkey in late Jan 2026. On about 12 Feb 2026 it returned via **WhaleCo Turkey** as official importer. Tax is paid in the cart, customs is handled by the company, rates are 5–20% by product group, the minimum basket is 580 TL, and shipping on those orders is free.
- **Trendyol.** No information found on cross-border sales.

### Cited Findings
- **AliExpress status.** AliExpress remains accessible, but 6 Feb 2026 brought "her paket … vergiye ve detaylı incelemeye" for everything. "AliExpress genellikle vergiyi sepet aşamasında tahsil etmektedir", though extra customs costs may still apply. The site also claims AliExpress is shifting to "Yerli Depo ve Lojistik Merkezleri" (Turkish-warehouse stock, about 2-day delivery, pricier) — [cospier.com, 27–29 Jan 2026](https://cospier.com/aliexpress-kapandi-mi.html) (blog; not verified).
- **AliExpress complaints, Feb–Mar 2026.** These are user claims, unverified, collected from [Şikayetvar AliExpress vergi ücreti](https://www.sikayetvar.com/aliexpress/vergi-ucreti), [gümrük vergisi](https://www.sikayetvar.com/aliexpress/vergi-ucreti/gumruk-vergisi) and [gümrük hizmet bedeli complaint](https://www.sikayetvar.com/aliexpress/aliexpress-gumruk-hizmet-bedeli-yuksekligi-nedeniyle-siparis-iptali-ve-iade-talebi) (via search snippets; the pages returned 403):
  - 1,263.93 TL tax was collected at checkout for a laser engraver, yet PTT still demanded customs tax on arrival.
  - About 5,500 TL "vergi beyanı" fee was demanded for a 1,400 TL signal lamp, with goods reportedly auctioned if unpaid.
  - An order placed on 6 Feb was stuck in customs as of 25 Mar after a "gümrük hizmet bedeli" demand.
  - An order of 3 Mar 2026: 54.11 TL item, 214.79 TL paid including "gümrük ve kargo", and the title mentions a later 5,634 TL extra demand. That figure equals exactly 3,984 + 1,650 TL, the PTT broker fee plus stamp duty from Q2.
  - Orders were closed after "gümrük muayenesi başarısız" without a refund.
- **Temu.** Temu removed cross-border products for Turkey in late January 2026 — [Euronews TR, 25 Jan 2026](https://tr.euronews.com/next/2026/01/25/temu-turkiyeye-yurtdisi-satislarini-kapatti). It then came back through WhaleCo:
  - Returned as "resmi ithalatçı" via WhaleCo: "vergiler alışveriş sırasında ödeniyor ve gümrük işlemleri şirket tarafından yürütülüyor".
  - "Yüzde 60 vergi yerine, ürün grubuna göre yüzde 5 ila yüzde 20 oranlar uygulanacak."
  - Minimum order 580 TL with free shipping. Battery/electrical items and clothing were initially excluded.
  - Source: [Euronews TR, 12 Feb 2026](https://tr.euronews.com/next/2026/02/12/temu-turkiyeye-dondu-gumruk-engelini-nasil-asti). Also [Technopat, 12 Feb 2026](https://www.technopat.net/2026/02/12/temu-yurt-disi-satisina-basladi/) and [Karar (580 TL alt limit)](https://www.karar.com/guncel-haberler/temu-turkiyede-sirket-kurarak-gumruk-sorununu-asti-alt-limit-580-tl-2028716).
- **Platform-importer exception.** The ETİD head said Temu "Türkiye'de depo kurdu ama sattığı her ürünü getirmesinin imkanı yok". He noted that domestic e-commerce sites importing goods would handle customs for customers — [Milliyet, 8 Jan 2026](https://www.milliyet.com.tr/ekonomi/yurt-disindan-e-ticarette-gumruksuz-donem-bitti-7517209).

### Inferences
- For watch parts, the safest import-like options are platforms where a **Turkish legal entity is importer of record** and tax is final at checkout: Temu/WhaleCo, AliExpress items stocked in Turkish warehouses, or Turkish resellers on Trendyol, Hepsiburada and N11. That avoids the ~5,600 TL per-parcel broker and stamp overhead.
- Plain cross-border AliExpress "vergi dahil" orders shipped by post carry a real risk of the full PTT broker overhead being added on arrival.
- Temu's catalogue includes some watch tools, straps and parts, but availability of specific movements or parts is not established here.

### Gaps
- No official AliExpress statement was found on its 2026 Turkey tax model: DDP, importer of record, or the "Choice" local-warehouse programme.
- No data was found on Trendyol's cross-border or global offering in 2026.
- It is not confirmed whether Temu's 5–20% is a tax rate or a platform-absorbed blended rate, or whether it still applies in Oct 2026.

## 5. JLCPCB / JLC3DP / JLCCNC and PCBWay: shipping methods, cost and time for ~100 g, DDP, and Turkish user experiences

### Takeaway
- **Methods.** JLCPCB and PCBWay ship to Turkey mainly by express couriers (DHL, FedEx, UPS; 3–7 business days), plus slower postal/airmail options.
- **Duties.** Neither publishes a Turkey DDP (tax-included) line. Duties are the buyer's responsibility, and Turkey requires a recipient ID or tax number.
- **Experience since Feb 2026.** Turkish users report that small JLCPCB parcels via DHL/FedEx needed full broker clearance, costing **~10,000–20,000 TL in fees on $20–25 orders**. Forums now advise local PCB makers or Turkish import intermediaries.

### Cited Findings
- **JLCPCB customs page** (last updated 9 Sep 2026):
  - "All duties and taxes for international shipments are the responsibility of the customer."
  - Turkey is listed among countries that "require an identification number".
  - The tax-collecting (VAT-at-checkout) setup is described only for the EU and UK.
  - Source: [JLCPCB — Customs, Duties and Taxes](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/customs,-duties-and-taxes).
- **JLCPCB methods and times** (undated FAQ): DHL about 3–5 business days, EMS 7–15, registered airmail 2–4 weeks. Cost depends on weight, destination and method, shown at checkout — [JLCPCB FAQ](https://jlcpcb.com/faqs) (via search snippet).
- **JLCPCB DDP.** Described as customs fees paid by JLC — [JLCPCB payment methods](https://cart.jlcpcb.com/client/template/payment/payMethod.html). The 2026 DDP and EU Customs Reform material covers EU VAT and duty only — [JLCPCB EUCR FAQ (Jul 2026)](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/eu-customs-reform-faq). There is no indication of a DDP line for Turkey.
- **"Global Standard Direct Line"** is described as DAP (duty and tax collected at delivery) in a Mar 2026 US thread — [All About Circuits](https://forum.allaboutcircuits.com/threads/jlcpcb-global-standard-direct-line-shipping.209788/). Its availability for Turkey is unknown. A ~3-year-old Turkish forum comment said tax was collected in advance on that line (via search snippet), which is outdated.
- **Turkish JLCPCB shipping prices** (anecdotal, 2025–2026): $19.80 (10 pcs), $20, $25 (5 pcs) for DHL/FedEx; $45 for a stencil plus PCB order — [prototippcb.com.tr Q&A](https://prototippcb.com.tr/415/jlcpcb-pcb-i%CC%87thalat-s%C3%BCreci). At 7 Oct 2026 TCMB USD selling 49.1976, $20–25 ≈ 984–1,230 TL.
- **Turkish experiences after Feb 2026.**
  - Fees: müşavir 5,200 TL, ardiye 4,800 TL, DHL 2,800 TL; totals of 10,000–20,000 TL on $20–25 orders and $700 paid on a $95 order — [prototippcb.com.tr](https://prototippcb.com.tr/415/jlcpcb-pcb-i%CC%87thalat-s%C3%BCreci).
  - Mekatronik.org staff (25 May 2026): at least about $300 even for free goods, with TSE/TAREKS risk.
  - Users (29 May 2026) list intermediaries that import on the customer's behalf (demsay, pcbgetir, megasan, vegapcb, serpcb, pcbreel) and a local PCB option (numunepcb/makropcb) — [mekatronik.org thread](https://mekatronik.org/forum/threads/jlcpcb-siparisi-ve-turkiyeye-gonderim-dhl-dds-gumruk-deneyimi.9648/).
  - A Feb 2024 DonanımHaber thread reported EMC/TAREKS paperwork costing about 50,000 TL for bare prototype PCBs — [DonanımHaber forum](https://forum.donanimhaber.com/bos-pcb-uretiminin-emc-ve-tarekse-tabi-oldugu-dogru-mu--158000879) (older, via search snippet).
- **PCBWay.**
  - Methods: DHL and UPS (3–7 business days), FedEx (older page: 4–7), Hong Kong Post, China Post Air Mail and ePacket (slower), and "PCBWayExpress" (8–16 business days, limited countries) — [PCBWay shipping methods help (updated Feb 2024)](https://www.pcbway.com/helpcenter/shipping_instructions/How_many_shipping_methods_on_pcbway_.html); [PCBWay shipping guide](https://www.pcbway.com/shipping_method_guide.aspx).
  - Customs duty is the buyer's responsibility, and Turkey is among countries where a tax-ID-like identifier is required — [PCBWay help: customs duty responsibility](https://www.pcbway.ru/helpcenter/delivery/Who_will_be_responsible_for_the_customs_duty_that_may_be_levied_at_my_country_.html) (via search snippet).
  - PCBWay reproduces DHL "Duty Tax Paid" billing, but there is no evidence it is offered for Turkey (via search snippet).
- **Third-party JLCPCB importer.** jlcpcb.tr (JETON Teknoloji Ltd. Şti.) claims delivery with customs duties paid, plus TSE/TAREKS for PCBA. It is a third party with commercial interest and its JLCPCB affiliation is unverified — [jlcpcb.tr](https://jlcpcb.tr/) (via search snippet).

### Inferences
- **Direct ordering is uneconomic.** JLC3DP (3D printed) and JLCCNC (CNC/metal) parts, such as watch cases, dials and spacers, ship from the same JLC logistics. They will hit the same regime: courier → individual → broker-cleared normal import.
- **Cost of a ~100 g parcel.** For a 5,000 TL watch build, a direct JLC or PCBWay order costs roughly 1,000–1,250 TL in shipping, plus about 5,600 TL (PTT, if a postal line is available) or 10,000+ TL (DHL/FedEx) in clearance costs. It is only sensible if many parts are consolidated, a Turkish intermediary consolidates with other buyers, or local fabrication is used.
- **Metal parts.** CNC-machined metal watch cases (Chapter 91, 9111) are less likely to trigger TAREKS than electronics, but this is not verified.

### Gaps
- I could not get live JLCPCB/PCBWay checkout quotes for a 100 g parcel to Turkey (needs a logged-in cart), so method availability and exact prices for Turkey in Oct 2026 are unverified.
- No JLC3DP- or JLCCNC-specific Turkey shipping pages were found.
- No Ekşi Sözlük or Reddit 2026 posts specific to JLCPCB/PCBWay customs in Turkey were found beyond the forums above.

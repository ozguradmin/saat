# Buying NH35 mod-watch parts in Turkey without the ~5,600 TL per-parcel customs overhead: domestic and importer-of-record channels (as of 7 Oct 2026)

**Scope and method.** All research was done on 2026-10-07. Trendyol, Hepsiburada, n11, Akakçe and Amazon.com.tr all returned HTTP 403 or 503 to both WebFetch and curl. Prices from those sites therefore come from **search-engine snippets** and are marked "(snippet)". Many snippets carry no capture date, so treat undated TL prices as "seen in index, date unknown". Temu product pages loaded, but showed no price (see Q1). The cost baseline comes from the sibling note `gumruk_kur_kargo.md`. Every non-medicine parcel to an individual now goes through formal clearance, with a typical PTT overhead of about 3,984 TL broker minimum plus about 1,500–1,650 TL stamp duty, roughly 5,600 TL in total. The figures below show which channels avoid that overhead.

**Warning about mirror sites.** Many Turkish-looking ".com.tr" domains show up in searches for NH35 parts. Examples are adnanelektrik.com.tr, vipotocekici.com.tr, recepgurkan.com.tr, okyanuselektrik.com.tr, casuskamera.com.tr, novaakademi.com.tr, progarage.com.tr, elenberg.com.tr, serkankara.com.tr, lavasdunyasi.com.tr, karacafuar.com.tr, kitapevim.com.tr and otobanlastiktamiri.com.tr. Their names have nothing to do with watches, and their URLs follow patterns like `/value_Branch_381760/` or `/Retail-219816_latest/`. I opened one of them (adnanelektrik). It showed:
- a machine-translated AliExpress listing ("Kaza Malzemesi", "Bozel Malzeme"), with origin "Anakara Çin";
- no company name (unvan), tax number or address;
- unrelated categories such as office furniture.

These should **not** be treated as domestic stock. If they work at all, they are scraped AliExpress or dropship listings. They would ship from China and expose the buyer to the same customs procedure — [adnanelektrik.com.tr listing, fetched 2026-10-07](https://www.adnanelektrik.com.tr/value_Branch_381760/).

## Q1. Temu Türkiye (WhaleCo importer model): does it sell NH35/NH38 movements, cases, hands, dial dots and tool kits to Turkey? Prices, minimum order, delivery

### Takeaway
Temu Türkiye runs as an **importer-of-record model**. WhaleCo Turkey Teknoloji A.Ş. is the importer, tax is paid in the cart, the minimum basket is 580 TL, and delivery takes about 7–20 days. This is the only foreign-catalogue channel found that structurally avoids the ~5,600 TL broker overhead. Temu's global catalogue does contain NH35 parts: green-lume NH35 hand sets, a 36 mm sapphire NH35 case, movement holders, case-opening tools and a 33.5 mm dial. **No NH35 movement listing on Temu was found.** I could not confirm that any of the NH35 parts can currently be bought on the Turkish storefront. All three Temu /tr/ watch-part pages I opened said "Satın alma işlemi şu anda mevcut değil". The only Turkish-storefront prices found are a 147-piece watch repair kit at 484 TL (Feb 2026) and an NH35 hand set at 82.23 TL via an aggregator (undated).

## Q2. AliExpress to Turkey in mid/late 2026: is tax collected at checkout with no further broker fee, or do buyers still pay ~5,600 TL on arrival?

### Takeaway
I found **no evidence** that AliExpress has an importer-of-record or full-DDP arrangement for Turkey in 2026. The newest dated buyer reports are April–May 2026. They show PTT and assigned brokers (notably **GND Global / Gündoğdu Global Gümrük Müşavirliği**) demanding about 5,500–6,000 TL per parcel, itemised as a ~3,984 TL clearance minimum plus ~1,500 TL stamp duty. This happened even when AliExpress had collected "vergi" at checkout. I found no June–October 2026 report showing the problem fixed. The working assumption should be that an AliExpress parcel to Turkey still risks the full ~5,600 TL overhead.

### Cited Findings
- **Order of 13 Apr 2026.** Three motorcycle indicator lamps worth about 1,400 TL. After entry, the buyer was told to make the tax declaration online through GND Global, and about 5,500 TL was demanded. The goods would be auctioned if unpaid and no refund was given — [Şikayetvar AliExpress vergi ücreti](https://www.sikayetvar.com/aliexpress/vergi-ucreti) (search summary; page 403).
- **GND Global complaints (40 listed on its Şikayetvar page; user claims):**
  - about 6,000 TL demanded for a ~600 TL geared motor;
  - "asgari gümrükleme bedeli 3,984 TL + 1,500 TL damga vergisi" on a ~600 TL parcel;
  - 9,800 TL claimed on a 1,440 TL item;
  - 10,514 TL on a 1,000 TL plastic accessory;
  - an AliExpress order with a "gümrük ödemesi talebi".
  
  One user instead paid only the statutory duty and stamp tax and collected the parcel via PTT — [Şikayetvar GND Global](https://www.sikayetvar.com/gnd-global-gumruk-musavirligi-ve-lojistik); [6000 TL complaint](https://www.sikayetvar.com/gnd-global-gumruk-musavirligi-ve-lojistik/gnd-global-gumruk-musavirligi-ve-lojistik-gumruk-bahane-edilen-6000-tl-ek-ucret-talebi); [AliExpress siparişimde gümrük ödemesi talebi](https://www.sikayetvar.com/gnd-global-gumruk-musavirligi-ve-lojistik/aliexpress-siparisimde-gumruk-odemesi-talebi-ve-yaniltici-uygulama); [1440 TL → 9800 TL](https://www.sikayetvar.com/gnd-global-gumruk-musavirligi-ve-lojistik/1440-tllik-urune-9800-tl-gumruk-masrafi-iddiasi) (all search summaries; Şikayetvar returns 403).
- **Trade Ministry complaint page.** A user reports a 6,000 TL declaration demand (undated) — [Şikayetvar Ticaret Bakanlığı gümrük](https://www.sikayetvar.com/ticaret-bakanligi/gumruk-bakanligi) (search summary).
- **Feb–Mar 2026 complaints (from the sibling note).** Tax was paid at checkout and PTT still demanded money. The 3 Mar 2026 order with a 5,634 TL extra demand is in `gumruk_kur_kargo.md` — [Şikayetvar gümrük vergisi](https://www.sikayetvar.com/aliexpress/vergi-ucreti/gumruk-vergisi).
- **Blog claims (unverified).** Cospier (Jan 2026) says AliExpress "genellikle vergiyi sepet aşamasında tahsil etmektedir" but extra customs costs may apply. It also says AliExpress is moving to Turkish-warehouse stock with about 2-day delivery at higher prices. No official AliExpress statement was found — [cospier.com](https://cospier.com/aliexpress-kapandi-mi.html).
- **Official fee framing.** The Ministry's FAQ says it only collects taxes. Transport, storage and other service fees are charged by the carrier — [ticaret.gov.tr](https://ticaret.gov.tr/gumruk-islemleri/sikca-sorulan-sorular/bireysel/posta-ve-hizli-kargo-muafiyeti) (search summary).
- **Possible policy rollback.** DonanımHaber ran a headline "AK Parti'den yurt dışı alışveriş düzenlemesinde geri adım sinyali". I saw only the headline and did not read the content — [DonanımHaber](https://www.donanimhaber.com/ak-parti-den-yurt-disi-alisveris-duzenlemesinde-geri-adim-sinyali--200796).

### Inferences
- Even a 54 TL AliExpress order can become a ~5,600 TL parcel. For a sub-5,000 TL build, AliExpress direct is not viable unless the listing ships from a Turkish warehouse ("yerel depo"/TR stock) sold by a Turkish entity.
- The broker overhead is per parcel. Consolidating every part into one order or one parcel limits it to one fee, but a single fee still exceeds the whole budget.

### Gaps
- No Jun–Oct 2026 AliExpress-to-Turkey buyer reports were found. Searches restricted to Şikayetvar, Ekşi, DonanımHaber and Technopat surfaced nothing newer than May 2026. Reddit cannot be reached by this tool.
- I could not confirm whether AliExpress offers TR-warehouse ("Yerel Depo") stock of NH35 parts.
- I could not confirm whether the rollback signal led to any change after Feb 2026. The sibling note `gumruk_kur_kargo.md` treats the regime as still in force on 7 Oct 2026.

## Q3. Hepsiburada / Trendyol / n11 / Amazon.com.tr cross-border ("yurt dışından") listings for NH35 parts: who pays duty in 2026, and are prices final?

### Takeaway
Hepsiburada's search index still shows NH35 listings tagged **"Yurt Dışından"**, for example:
- a Talent NH35A movement at **1,581.36 TL**;
- Humble NH35/NH36 silver/green-lume hands, **3 sets for 580.78 TL**.

Hepsiburada historically ran these by calculating customs in the cart and clearing them itself. I found no 2026 statement confirming this continues, or that these listings are still purchasable after 6 Feb 2026. On Amazon.com.tr, the help page states that since 6 Feb 2026 only **books** can be ordered through the individual-import channel. Global Store or "ships from outside Turkey" watch parts are therefore effectively closed. Trendyol shows complete NH35 watches from Turkish sellers, but no cross-border parts were found. Domestic Trendyol and Hepsiburada resale prices for imported gadgets rose 2–13× after Feb 2026.

### Inferences
- **The donor-watch route does not fit a 5,000 TL budget with new domestic stock.** The cheapest domestic new NH35 watch found is about 7,049 TL (Pagani), and the cheapest Seiko 5 SRPD is about 9,900 TL, with a one-day low of about 7,457 TL.
- It could fit only through:
  - a second-hand Pagani, Guanqin or similar NH35 watch (2,500–3,000 TL in 2024 forum prices, probably higher now);
  - a Temu TR complete NH35 watch, if one is listed under WhaleCo with tax at checkout.
- A donor gives a matched movement, crown/stem, spacer, case, crystal and strap in one customs-free purchase. The buyer then needs only a custom 28.5 mm dial and (optionally) hands.
- Pagani "homage" cases often use dials smaller or larger than 28.5 mm. A Seiko 5 SRPD (4R36) takes standard 28.5 mm Seiko-pattern mod dials but is 42.5 mm, outside the 36–40 mm target. The dial-size and case-size claims here are general mod-community knowledge, not verified in this session. See `teknik_olculer.md`.
